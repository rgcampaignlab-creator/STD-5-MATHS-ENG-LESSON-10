Notes

📏 Measuring Boundaries - Perimeter

The length of thread needed to go around a rectangle is called its perimeter.

💡 Perimeter of a rectangle = 2 × (length + width)

🟩 Measuring Insides - Area

The measure of the space inside a shape is called its area. A square of side 1 cm has area 1 square centimetre.

💡 Area of a rectangle = length × width

Two rectangles can have the same perimeter but different areas!

🧩 Other Shapes (L-shapes)

  • An L-shaped figure can be split into two rectangles - add their areas to get the total area.
  • Alternatively, an L-shape can be seen as a big rectangle with a smaller rectangle cut off - subtract the areas.
  • When we cut a small rectangle off a corner, the area decreases but the perimeter stays the same.

Model Question Paper

Model Question Paper - Maths

Class 5 | Lesson 10: Rectangle Math | Time: 45 minutes

  1. Find the area of a rectangle with length 6 cm and width 3 cm.
  2. Find the perimeter of a rectangle with sides 5 cm and 3 cm.
  3. Fill in the blank: The perimeter of a rectangle is twice the _____ of its sides.
  4. Fill in the blank: The area of a rectangle is the _____ of the lengths of its sides.
  5. A rectangle has area 12 sq.cm and perimeter 14 cm. Find its length and width.
  6. Explain why cutting a small rectangle off a corner of a bigger rectangle can reduce the area but keep the perimeter the same.
  7. Find the area of a rectangle: 12 cm × 5 cm.
  8. Find the perimeter of a rectangle: 11 m and 7 m.
  9. What is the area of a square of side 10 cm?
  10. Two rectangles have the same perimeter of 14 cm. Give two different possible sets of sides.

Answers:

  1. 18 sq.cm
  2. 16 cm
  3. sum
  4. product
  5. 6 cm and 2 cm (or similar pair)
  6. Because the cut edges add back the same total length that was removed, but reduce the enclosed space
  7. 60 sq.cm
  8. 36 m
  9. 100 sq.cm
  10. e.g. 6 cm × 1 cm, and 5 cm × 2 cm
